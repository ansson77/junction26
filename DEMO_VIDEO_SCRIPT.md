# Demo video script (2:00)

Thesis: **AI helps people describe themselves. Humans make every decision.**

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A pile of near-identical CVs, then an AI screening dashboard | "Today, AI writes the CVs, and AI reads them. At Espoo's recruitment events, that means hundreds of polished applications nobody can tell apart." |
| 0:15 | Logo + thesis | "In our service, AI helps people *describe* themselves, but humans make every decision." |
| 0:22 | Aino uploads CV, ESCO skills appear | "Aino uploads her CV, in any language. We turn it into EU-standard ESCO skills, and she keeps, removes or adds each one." |
| 0:37 | 10-point allocation → validation question | "Then she spends 10 points on what she's really best at. An AI can polish a CV, but it can't make that choice for her. She answers one short question in her own words, and recruiters see it exactly as she wrote it." |
| 0:57 | Business-ID → ideas → tasks → employer points | "Many companies don't know what to hire for. They enter a Business-ID, answer a few questions, and we suggest concrete ideas based on their real situation, turned into tasks and skills. They get the same 10-point budget, so they decide what matters *before* the event." |
| 1:15 | Ranked list, open one match explanation | "Then the payoff: everyone at the event, ranked by fit, with every match explained. No black box. The recruiter reads the answers and chooses who to meet." |
| 1:35 | Follow-up email + Espoo resources | "Afterwards, nobody is left hanging. Applicants who weren't picked get feedback and a path to Espoo's resources for next time." |
| 1:48 | Closing card with EU AI Act on screen | "AI helps people figure out their needs, but humans stay in control." |

## Recording notes

- Use one named applicant (Aino) and one named company throughout.
- Prepare the seeded data before recording so nobody waits for loading screens. Speed up or cut every transition.
- Show each screen only long enough to land one idea. Never narrate the UI controls.
- If you have a number, put it on screen (for example, "13,955 ESCO skills").
- If you go over time, trim the 0:57 company section first.
