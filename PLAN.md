# Here is our vision:


Website that matches applicants and employers based on real job
requirements and applicant self ranked skills.


## Employers side:

1. Employer opens the website. They can either enter a job application
they have ready, or in a freeform way enter tasks they are looking
for someone to do.

2. They are asked clarifying questions based on previous step,
with the aim of making clear tasks the potential employee will be
expected to complete.

3. These are matched autonomously to ESCO skills. These skills are
then presented to the recruiter, and they rank them.

4. Done.

## Applicant side:

1. Applicant enters their linkedin or CV.

2. ESCO skills are suggested from their text. These are only
suggestions: the applicant confirms, removes or adds every skill.

3. Applicant must choose a set amount of skills (5), and they get 10
points to allocate to each one based on their expertise.

4. 1-2 of the most weighted skills gets a validation question ("describe
one time you used this skill"). The question is generated from the ESCO
skill only, never from applicant data. The applicant's answer is shown
verbatim to recruiters and is never passed to an LLM.

**AI Act principle:** our AI never evaluates applicants (Annex III
4(a), high-risk). AI helps applicants describe themselves and helps
employers describe jobs; all judging of people is done by humans.
Profiles are pure self-declaration.

5. Employees are shortlisted by their skills, their weighting of them.
Additional options like visa-eligibility can be added. The matching is
a transparent rule over self-declared skills and points (no AI
scoring), and recruiters can see why each person matched.

6. Event happens.

7. Employee gets feedback based on their own skill ranking, and what
employees were looking for.

8. Done.
