# Here is our vision:


Website that matches applicants and employers based on real job
requirements and applicant self ranked skills.


## Emploers side:

1. Employer opens the website. They can either enter a job application
they have ready, or in a freeform way enter tasks they are looking
for someone to do.

2. They are asked clarifying questions based on previous step,
with the aim of making clear tasks the potential employee will be
expected to complete.

3. These are matched autonomously to ESCO skills. These skills are
then presented to the recruiter, and they rank them.

4. Done.

## Applicant side:

1. Applicant enters their linkedin or CV.

2. Their ESCO skills are extracted. Applicant can correct or add.

3. Applicant is grilled on some of the skills: what evidence is
there?

4. Applicant must choose a set amount of skills, and they get 10(ish) points to allocate to each one based on their expertise.

5. Employees are shortlisted by their skills, their weighting of them.
Additional options like visa-eligibility can be added.

6. Event happens.

7. Employee gets feedback based on their own skill ranking, and what
employees were looking for.

8. Done.
